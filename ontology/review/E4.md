## ONTO-2 final cold review — 2026-10-08

Implementer: Codex. Independent reviewer: GPT-6-astra, fresh isolated agent.
**reviewed by model, not by a domain expert**. Reviewer read only numbered masked
inputs under `blind-inputs/onto2-blind-*.ttl`; no rule IDs, source text, repository
context or other worktree. Static reading only; executable evidence is separate.
Status remains **proposed**. Comparison below is by the implementer after review.
Input 1 (and final addendum after tightening the flag).

Reviewer read: each Persona has at least three distinct card nodes, each with
nonblank quote/locator; optional attributes require both too. Exactly one platform,
exact AI label, and exactly one identityMode. author-ids needs five distinct string
IDs and forbids unverified; unverified-content forbids IDs, needs five distinct
content strings and exactly one exact flag. Named guarded shapes identify the
failed mode. Initial review found extra contradictory flags allowed; maxCount 1
and a negative fixture repaired that, and the final masked input was re-reviewed.

Source comparison: thresholds, alternatives and required labels agree within the
metadata projection. Every author is attached by `persona ex:author ID` and every
card by `persona ex:card cardNode`; counts are per focus persona. Foreign-author
and foreign-card controls prove another persona's links do not contribute.

ESCALATED: this does not map a card to its individual author or prove quote
independence, platform provenance, exact source bytes or semantic support. Three
card nodes may repeat one quote. That is not prohibited by the source's numeric
card condition alone. Five literal IDs do not prove five people. Group size x/y,
self-reported demographics and placement at the head of the rendered persona are
outside this bounded projection. A real importer and evidence review are needed;
adding a guessed card-to-author relationship would not authenticate the evidence.

Model change: identityMode is mandatory: author-ids or unverified-content.
E4AuthorIDs and E4UnverifiedContent appear as sourceShape in negative verdicts.
Deleted E4-duplicate-author.ttl and its manifest row: RDF collapses repeated
literals, making it identical to E4-four-authors.ttl.

Whitespace review: the range pattern rejects Unicode White_Space and ASCII
control-only text; it does not normalize text or exclude every invisible format
character (U+200B/U+FEFF remain allowed). The verified checker refuses escaped
tab/newline/CR data rather than judging it. The mixed-whitespace fixture retains
expected REJECT and is explicitly ESCALATED, never counted as passing.
