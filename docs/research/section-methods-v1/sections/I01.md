# I01 · Câu hỏi kinh doanh

Spec v1.0.0 · coverage `DRAFTED` · authority `OWNER_INPUT_REQUIRED` · implementation `HUMAN_INPUT_AND_VALIDATION` · readiness `NOT_ASSESSED` · execution/review `NOT_RUN`.

## Question and boundary

What question should the research answer, for which decision and scope? The method captures an owner-authored brief and checks required fields. It cannot infer the owner's problem, intended use, audience or success definition from the report catalog.

## Inputs and procedure

Required owner fields: `questionText`, `decisionToInform`, `intendedAudience`, `scope` (market/entity/geography/time if applicable), and `knownConstraints`; owner may explicitly mark a field `UNSET`. Optional fields: desired evidence families, decision owner, deadline, success criteria, exclusions and review point. Values are text/versioned declarations, not evidence claims.

1. Validate that the question is one explicit, answerable sentence or preserve the exact question without rewriting its meaning. No new scope is inferred.
2. Validate that decision/audience/scope fields are supplied or explicitly `UNSET`; record unresolved parts. Do not force every report section to answer the question.
3. Emit exact owner text, brief version/digest, missing fields and candidate section IDs only when the owner selects them. Candidate section mapping is a navigation aid, not a method dependency or inclusion mandate.
4. Reopen brief when owner edits question, decision, scope, audience or constraints. Preserve older brief version for existing reports.

No AI-generated objective, timeline, success threshold, decision owner or audience. Missing is not “all market” or current-year default. Ties not applicable.

## Output, example and failure

Output: `businessQuestionBrief` with exact owner values, `UNSET` markers, selected section references, version/digest and review state. Toy fixture: owner writes “Which existing channel records a compatible measured outcome in period P?” Scope and period remain owner-supplied; the tool stores the question unchanged.

Blocked fixture: no owner question is present. Expected `I01_OWNER_QUESTION_REQUIRED`; emit an empty brief template with unset fields, not a generated business question.

Allowed: “Owner brief version V asks Q, with scope fields A/B unresolved.” Forbidden: claiming research answered a question the owner did not ask, expanding “calcium” to all categories, or selecting a strategic objective.

AI role: none for authorship. A separate assistant may help edit only when explicitly requested and must return owner-confirmable wording; no factual claims. Human owner writes/approves the brief. Dependencies: none; upstream methods must not require I01 to calculate. Downstream M01/M11/M12/I14/I15 may use exact brief when supplied.

Open decisions: exact owner question, decision, audience, scope, constraints, success criteria and intended use. Proposed blockers: `I01_OWNER_QUESTION_REQUIRED`, `I01_DECISION_UNSET`, `I01_SCOPE_UNSET`, `I01_REVIEW_REQUIRED`. Reopen when the owner changes the brief. Acceptance: missing question stays empty; unset scope stays explicit; no unrelated section is made mandatory by section mapping.
