# M11 · Cơ hội

Spec v1.0.0 · coverage `DRAFTED` · authority `PROPOSED_EVIDENCE_INVENTORY; PRIORITY_RULE_UNRESOLVED` · implementation `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED` · readiness `NOT_ASSESSED` · execution/review `NOT_RUN`.

## Question and boundary

Which exact source-bound claims are relevant to the owner's opportunity question, and what evidence or counterevidence would be needed to judge them? M11 can compile an unranked opportunity-evidence inventory. It cannot name a verified market opportunity, size it, rank it or recommend investment without owner policy and compatible outcome evidence.

## Inputs and procedure

Required: owner objective/question; exact reviewed claims relevant to the question (for example compatible demand/supply/context observations); claim layer, scope, period, unit/denominator, limitations and source pointers; counterevidence. Optional owner-authored opportunity hypotheses, criteria or priority policy must be labeled as owner inputs. No claim is accepted from an unreviewed AI narrative alone.

1. Replay claim references and verify relevance against owner-supplied question tags; without tags, keep entries as general evidence, not opportunity support.
2. Group by exact owner hypothesis if one exists; otherwise group only by catalog source section. Retain support, contradiction, missing evidence and limitations. Keep distinct proxies/units/periods separate; do not manufacture a common score.
3. For each group emit unranked `evidence_relevant`, `counterevidence`, `unresolved_requirements` and exact pointers. Avoid the noun “validated opportunity” unless an owner-approved definition and all its evidence gates pass.
4. If policy exists, a human can record a separate evaluation decision with the applicable criteria and outcome. The recipe does not create or choose that decision.

Dedup exact claim ID+digest only. No weights, scores, opportunity-size interpolation, default rank, thresholds or assumption of actionability. UNKNOWN and missing stay visible. Evidence from one group does not represent a population without a valid frame.

## Output, example and failure

Output: question/hypothesis ref, exact claim refs, scope compatibility, supporting and conflicting items, missing evidence, owner criteria ref, `rank=null` absent policy, and disposition. Toy fixture: synthetic claim A says a located source reports interest; claim B says a comparable source reports a constraint. Expected both appear in one unranked evidence group; neither becomes an opportunity score.

Blocked fixture: only sales volume and category co-occurrence exist, with no demand proxy authorization or outcome compatibility. Expected `M11_OPPORTUNITY_EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT`; preserve those measures under their original names.

Allowed: “These claims may bear on the owner-defined question; evidence is mixed/limited.” Forbidden: opportunity size, “best opportunity”, expected return, probability, or action recommendation absent review.

AI role: optional claim-ID selection/short draft; the separate proposed candidate contract may draft unranked evidence-linked hypotheses for human review, never facts, scores or decisions. Human owner defines opportunity and priority policy. Dependencies: only exact relevant claims, potentially M05/M06/M07/M09/I09/I13; unrelated section incompleteness does not block inventory. Open decisions: opportunity definition, eligibility, comparability, criteria, weights, risk and priority. Blockers: `M11_OWNER_OBJECTIVE_MISSING`, `M11_CLAIM_NOT_ELIGIBLE`, `M11_PROXY_UNAPPROVED`, `M11_PRIORITY_POLICY_MISSING`. Reopen on objective/evidence/policy changes. Acceptance: no ranking without policy; counterevidence is retained; absent one unrelated section does not block source inventory.

Optional proposed AI path: use the common-rules AI proposal contract, unexecuted and unapproved in A40. Keep evidence-linked candidates in a separate layer-three list with exact fact references, concise rationale, assumptions, counterevidence and limits. They are not owner-authored options, facts or decisions. No ranks/scores or automatic action. Human disposition is separate; existing button-only B7/B10 contracts acquire no new required reason fields.
