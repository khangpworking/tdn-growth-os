# I07 · Lý do lựa chọn

Spec v1.0.0 · coverage `DRAFTED` · authority `PROPOSED_STATED_REASON_METHOD` · implementation `NEW_CODE_IF_APPROVED` · readiness `NOT_ASSESSED` · execution/review `NOT_RUN`.

## Question and boundary

What reason does an existing source explicitly give for an identified choice, and what choice/outcome does the source link it to? This is a located-account method. Purchase or click does not reveal motive; researchers must not infer “reason” from product attributes or likely intent.

## Inputs and procedure

Required: existing source corpus, owner question, exact `EvidenceRecordRef`, explicit choice among source-described options, explicit stated reason, context and available result/selection. An approved reason codebook is required for categories. `caseId`/person ID is optional; use only source-issued keys. No new interview or survey is proposed.

1. Freeze exact source records. Extract the stated choice, options if present, reason wording and whether an outcome occurred; link each value to its own span. Mark `reasonNotStated` separately from missing source text.
2. Classify only direct statements under an approved codebook. Keep multi-reason statements multi-coded only if policy allows; otherwise flag for adjudication, not forced into one category.
3. Compare reason and choice only inside the same source-linked record/episode. Without stable identity, retain independent case excerpts. Do not count person frequency or infer preference share.
4. Summarize located records by codebook category and evidence coverage. Include counterexamples, contradictions and explicit unknowns. Do not assign importance weights or rank reasons.

Compatible coding unit and context are required. Only repeated exact evidenceRecordRef values or a real stable source record key may deduplicate copies; identical wording does not. No cross-source joining without a real shared identity. No denominator or percentage unless a documented compatible respondent unit/frame exists and owner authorizes the claim.

## Output, example and limits

Output: record locator, choice, stated reason, context, result/selection status, codebook/adjudication, direct-vs-unknown flags, counterevidence and count unit. Toy fixture: an existing located account says “selected option A because delivery was faster”; expected one stated reason tied to that source account, no general preference statistic.

Blocked fixture: a listing shows a large pack and sold count, but no choice account or stated reason. Expected `I07_REASON_NOT_STATED`; no inference that price/pack caused selection.

Allowed: “This source account states reason R for choice C.” Cite exact spans. Forbidden: purchase motive, customer preference ranking, prevalence, representative population claim or causal driver from selection alone.

AI role: may propose matching an explicit reason to an approved codebook, with citation; coder/adjudicator accepts/rejects. It cannot synthesize reasons. Required source context and evidence refs may optionally reuse I02/M13. I04 outcome is optional only when the owner asks about result. Open choices: reason taxonomy, multiple-code rule, outcome/choice evidence standard, coding/adjudication and any denominator. Proposed blockers: `I07_CHOICE_NOT_LOCATED`, `I07_REASON_NOT_STATED`, `I07_CODEBOOK_UNAPPROVED`, `I07_RESPONDENT_DENOMINATOR_MISSING`. Reopen when corpus, coding or source context changes. Acceptance: no reason assigned from purchase alone; reason and choice pointers resolve separately; multi-code ambiguity remains flagged.

Record identity: apply the common-rules record-identity policy. Collapse only repeated exact evidenceRecordRef values or records proven identical by a stable source key. Identical wording at different row/span locators remains distinct; text hashes alone never authorize record deduplication. Corpus counts are coded records, not people.
