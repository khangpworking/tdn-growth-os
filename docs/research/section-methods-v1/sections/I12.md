# I12 · Điểm tiếp xúc

Spec v1.0.0 · coverage `DRAFTED` · authority `PROPOSED_EXPOSURE_OUTCOME_METHOD; EFFECTIVENESS_BLOCKED` · implementation `NEW_CODE_IF_APPROVED` · readiness `NOT_ASSESSED` · execution/review `NOT_RUN`.

## Question and boundary

Which touchpoints are documented, and which already measured outcomes can be linked to compatible exposure records? Listing a channel does not show reach; clicks do not show purchase; a purchase after exposure does not prove effectiveness. No campaign or customer experiment is proposed here.

## Inputs and procedure

Required for inventory: exact touchpoint source/locator, date, source-reported channel/content and provenance. Required for a rate: compatible exposure denominator, outcome numerator, same unit/population, period, attribution window, dedup rule and stable linking key or source-provided aggregate. Causal/effectiveness claims additionally require a valid comparison/assignment design and predeclared analysis.

1. Freeze channel/touchpoint taxonomy and corpus. Use source-reported touchpoint; do not infer exposure from a listing or content item.
2. Preserve exposure events and outcomes separately. Link only via stable source-supported key or exact compatible aggregate; manual locators are evidence refs, not person-level joins. Deduplicate only under an approved event/unit key.
3. Partition by channel, outcome definition, population, window and period. Compute an observed rate only where `outcome / exposed eligible units` is meaningful, exact and denominator-compatible; zero denominator returns null. Keep exposure count, outcome count, missing instrumentation and unlinked records visible.
4. Describe association as measured association. Any effectiveness/cause estimate must consume an independently authorized experiment/identification design, not this inventory; I16 design/result remains a distinct method.

Without attribution window or assignment, no effectiveness claim. Missing instrumentation stays missing, not zero exposure/outcome. No channel ranking or multi-touch credit allocation is automatic.

## Output, example and failure

Output: touchpoint ref, channel/content code, exposure state/count/unit, outcome definition/count, compatible denominator/window, link basis, exact rate if permitted, unlinked/missing counts and limits. Toy fixture: an existing aggregate reports 100 eligible exposures and 5 linked outcomes under the same stated window. Expected descriptive observed rate 5/100=5% if owner has approved this metric; no causal claim.

Blocked fixture: 100 impressions and 5 monthly store orders have no compatible unit, period or attribution link. Expected `I12_EXPOSURE_OUTCOME_INCOMPATIBLE`; no 5% rate/effectiveness claim.

Allowed: “The source reports X linked outcomes among Y eligible exposures under window W.” Forbidden: touchpoint caused outcomes, channel ROI, effectiveness ranking or attribution to a person without real stable IDs and design.

AI role: none for linkage, attribution, rate or ranking. Human approves taxonomy, exposure/outcome unit, window, dedup and design. Requires exposure/outcome primitives directly. May optionally reuse I13 content metadata, M13 provenance, or an existing human-reviewed I16 result; no experiment result is required for a located touchpoint inventory. Open choices: channel taxonomy, exposure unit, attribution window, join key, dedup and causal design. Blockers: `I12_EXPOSURE_MISSING`, `I12_OUTCOME_MISSING`, `I12_ATTRIBUTION_POLICY_MISSING`, `I12_LINK_INCOMPATIBLE`, `I12_ASSIGNMENT_DESIGN_MISSING`. Reopen on source or policy changes. Acceptance: no proxy for exposure/outcome; incompatible windows do not divide; no effectiveness from temporal order alone.
