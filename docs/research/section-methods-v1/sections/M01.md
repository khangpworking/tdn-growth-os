# M01 · Kết luận chính

Spec v1.0.0 · coverage `DRAFTED` · authority `PROPOSED_SOURCE_BOUND_SYNTHESIS; CONCLUSION_RULE_UNRESOLVED` · implementation `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED` · readiness `NOT_ASSESSED` · execution/review `NOT_RUN`.

## Question and boundary

What exact reviewed evidence is relevant to the supplied business question, and what conflicts or limits remain? M01 is a downstream summary. It never supplies an input needed to calculate its upstream sections. Without an owner question and reviewed decision policy, its safe output is an unranked evidence inventory, not a preferred conclusion.

## Inputs and procedure

Required to build inventory: exact eligible upstream claim refs with artifact IDs/versions/digests/pointers, source/calculation/AI/human layer, scope/denominator/limitations, current review disposition and conflict/counterevidence links. Optional: owner question, audience, decision context, and owner-approved synthesis/prioritization policy. A claim's exact referenced artifacts must replay. Unreviewed AI interpretations and method-only metadata cannot be labeled accepted evidence.

1. Verify each claim ref and retain missing/zero/UNKNOWN, method and population limits. Select only claims explicitly marked relevant to the supplied question; absent relevance rules, group by canonical section order and do not infer importance.
2. Deduplicate exact same `claimId + artifact digest` only. Preserve contradictions as parallel entries with source/dependency details; do not majority-vote, pick newest, score confidence or combine overlapping ALL/WIDE/CORE scopes.
3. Emit a compact inventory: question fields, exact claim, evidence layer, source/calculation status, scope/denominator, review, counterevidence, limitations, and unresolved decision. Do not summarize beyond claim meaning.
4. Only an authorized human with explicit objective, criteria, tradeoffs and review authority may author a conclusion/priority; record it as a separate user decision referencing the inventory. AI output stays separate and unapproved.

No missing claim is treated as negative evidence. No arbitrary sorting beyond catalog order then claim ID. Numerical statements copy verified facts only, with exact citations.

## Output, example and failure

Output: `question`, ordered claim inventory, contradictions, evidence gaps, exact citations, limitations, `decisionPolicyRef` or null, and `conclusionState=UNRANKED_INVENTORY|HUMAN_DECISION_RECORDED`. Synthetic example: two compatible claims report the same observed subtotal; output both exact refs and scopes; no conclusion is written without policy/owner review.

Blocked example: purported claim points to a missing Result digest. Expected `M01_CLAIM_REPLAY_FAILED`; exclude it from claim inventory but show the integrity blocker, not an invented value.

Allowed without policy: “These exact reviewed claims and counterclaims are available for the question.” Forbidden: preferred conclusion, executive ranking, market opportunity, recommended action, causality or decision attribution to AI.

AI role: at most a separately versioned draft summary selecting from validated claim IDs under a reviewed output contract; no new facts, ranking or action. Human review required. Dependencies: any relevant upstream claim, not all 29 sections; downstream is owner briefing only. Open decisions: owner question, relevance filter, weighting/prioritization, conflict handling, confidence rubric, decision authority. Proposed blockers: `M01_OWNER_QUESTION_MISSING`, `M01_CLAIM_REPLAY_FAILED`, `M01_SYNTHESIS_POLICY_UNAPPROVED`, `M01_HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED`. Reopen on claim/policy/question change. Acceptance: a missing unrelated section does not block inventory; incompatible claims remain separate; no policy yields no ranking.

Optional proposed AI path: use the common-rules AI proposal contract, unexecuted and unapproved in A40. Keep evidence-linked candidates in a separate layer-three list with exact fact references, concise rationale, assumptions, counterevidence and limits. They are not owner-authored options, facts or decisions. No ranks/scores or automatic action. Human disposition is separate; existing button-only B7/B10 contracts acquire no new required reason fields.
