# M04 · Cơ cấu thị trường

Spec v1.0.0 · coverage `DRAFTED` · authority `EXISTING_BOUNDED_SUBMETHOD` · implementation `EXISTS_METRIC_SCOPE_V1_AND_A27_CHARTS` · readiness `NOT_ASSESSED` · execution/review `NOT_RUN`.

## Question and boundary

How are the selected observed rows distributed across frozen label groups and shops, and how sensitive is the same-period concentration to removing the leading shop? The existing A1 calculator supplies the bounded arithmetic and A27 supplies its fixed visualization contract. This is not a market-share estimate, product-count result, causal account, or full market structure method.

A32's M03 summary alone does not contain the full M04 arrays. |

No second period or competitor population is implicit. The recipe uses the exact Result's frozen policy; prepared A38 binds A30/A31 and retained A32 M03 to the same normalized input. This is single-period composition. A missing or invalid label is output as the calculator's `UNKNOWN` group in ALL and creates a label issue; WIDE/CORE are blocked when labels have issues. A valid label's supplied `group` is used even when its classification is `UNKNOWN`.

## Compatibility and procedure

1. Replay the exact full A1 Result and packet; verify A27 ChartData derives from those bytes and claims. In prepared assembly, separately replay A30/A31/A37 and apply A38's exact workspace/package/input/catalog/M03 lineage checks. The A32 VerifiedMetricSet is not the full M04 data payload.
2. Read the full Result's `scopes[*].groups`, `concentration` and `withoutTopShop`; do not recompute these in charting. The existing calculator groups rows by each valid frozen label's `group` value, or `UNKNOWN` when label is missing/invalid. Group subtotals retain observed value/missing/non-exact counts. A group share uses exact `group revenue / scope revenue`; it is null unless scope revenue is complete/non-null and denominator nonzero. Percent display uses exact integer ratio and half-even 2 decimals.
3. Existing shop aggregation groups by canonical `[platform, shopId]`; it orders descending by observed revenue subtotal, then ascending code-unit canonical `shopKey` on ties. For K ∈ {1,3,10}, the stored numerator is the first `min(K, shopCount)` shop revenues and denominator is full-scope revenue. The Result retains exact numerator, denominator and rounded percentage only when comparable. Missing revenue stays missing; when scope revenue is incomplete no group/top-K share is emitted. An observed zero denominator also yields null ratio.
4. Existing sensitivity removes the deterministic leading shop from the same scope and stores remaining totals/concentration and `remainingRevenueShare = remaining observed revenue / original full-scope revenue`. A27 copies exact values and pointers to its fixed chart views and validates ordering, axis, geometry and limits. More than 100 groups blocks only that group-composition visual; it never truncates the underlying Result.

Dedup is inherited from normalized unique `[platform, shopId, listingId]` keys. Result groups use code-unit order; the shop tie-break is defined above. Missing remains missing, observed zero contributes zero. Partial observed subtotals may be shown but cannot produce group/top-K shares when the scope has any missing revenue. Ratios use percentage half-even 2 decimal places.

## Output, example and failure

Output fields from the exact Result include per-scope groups, each group's listing count/revenue/units/share, `concentration` at K=1/3/10, and `withoutTopShop`; A27 ChartSpec adds no calculation. Synthetic example: scope revenue 100 VND, Group A 60, Group B 40, two shops with revenues 60 and 40. Expected shares 60.00% and 40.00%; Top-1 60.00%; Top-3 uses two shops and is 100.00%; tie behavior is not exercised. This says nothing about market coverage.

Blocked example: one of the two rows has missing revenue. Expected observed subtotal 60 with one missing; group share and concentration are null, not `60/60=100%`. Stale/missing labels block WIDE/CORE. If there are over 100 groups, A27 blocks the view without combining categories.

## Claims, review and dependencies

Allowed: “Among this exact selected scope, group G has X observed revenue of complete denominator Y (p%).” Cite full Result group pointer, scope pointer, exact numerator, denominator, membership and source locators. Forbidden: market share without a verified population; growth; additive scope comparisons; quality/competitive strength; full representativeness; causal/effectiveness claim.

AI role: none for grouping, ranking, share or ChartSpec. A human reviews label/codebook validity and what “market structure” means. Depends on M03's exact prepared calculation; A27 presentation consumes these result fields. Does not require M01.

Proposed blockers: `M04_RESULT_INVALID`, `M04_DENOMINATOR_INCOMPLETE`, `M04_LABELS_UNRESOLVED`, `M04_CHART_GROUP_LIMIT`. Reopen when result, codebook, policy, membership, source, group order or denominator changes. Acceptance: ties resolve by canonical shopKey (calculator owns); incomplete denominator yields null shares (calculator owns); >100 groups blocks only the A27 group-composition visual (A27 owns), while exact Result arithmetic remains intact. Existing tests remain the primary owners.
