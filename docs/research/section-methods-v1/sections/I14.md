# I14 · Hướng cơ hội

Spec v1.0.0 · coverage `DRAFTED` · authority `PROPOSED_UNRANKED_OPPORTUNITY_SYNTHESIS` · implementation `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED` · readiness `NOT_ASSESSED` · execution/review `NOT_RUN`.

## Question and boundary

Which owner-defined opportunity directions have exact relevant evidence, conflicts and unanswered questions? This downstream synthesis can prepare an unranked portfolio of evidence bundles. It cannot select a preferred direction or infer market demand/return.

## Inputs and procedure

Required: owner-supplied objective/decision question and any owner-defined direction labels; exact eligible claim refs with source, scope, period, denominator, confidence/limitations and review status; explicit conflicts/counterevidence. Optional: owner-authored alternatives and evaluation criteria. No direction labels or priorities are inferred from section titles. Optional AI-drafted directions belong to a separate unreviewed candidate list, not owner declarations.

1. Replay each claim's source/result/pointer. Exclude invalid or unreviewed claims from decision-support status, while retaining the exclusion reason in inventory.
2. In the base inventory, attach each claim only to an exact owner-defined direction or keep it unassigned. Separately proposed AI candidates may cite those same exact claims but remain unreviewed and unranked. If multiple directions could fit, keep an ambiguity record; do not auto-resolve.
3. For each direction, list supporting evidence, counterevidence, compatible measures, open parameters, missing evidence and exact claim digest. Use only evidence directly relevant under owner question; unrelated section gaps are not blockers.
4. Emit unranked directions in owner order or stable text order. Leave `priority=null`. A human may record a separately authorized decision using approved criteria; the recipe does not score or choose.

No weights, normalized score, risk/return estimate, threshold, TAM or roadmap. Incompatible periods/universes remain separate. Missing is not negative evidence; a source may support a direction without establishing actionability.

## Output, example and limits

Output: direction label, exact claims, support/counterevidence, compatibility partitions, evidence coverage/limits, open questions, `priority=null` and human decision ref or null. Toy fixture: two owner-defined directions each have one supporting claim and one unresolved denominator. Expected both listed unranked with denominator blocker.

Blocked fixture: a proposed direction appears only in an unreviewed AI paragraph without source claim. Expected `I14_NO_ELIGIBLE_CLAIM`; retain as excluded draft, not evidence.

Allowed: “Direction D has these relevant claims and these unresolved points.” Forbidden: preferred direction, forecast return, market priority, score or recommendation absent owner criteria and review.

AI role: optional evidence-bounded grouping/draft from exact validated claim IDs; human decides relevance and direction. Depends on I01 objective and selected M11/I09/M05/M06/M07/I13/I16 claims as supplied; no requirement that unrelated sections be complete. Open choices: opportunity definition, grouping, conflict policy, criteria, weights, risk, owner and review. Proposed blockers: `I14_OBJECTIVE_MISSING`, `I14_DIRECTION_UNDEFINED`, `I14_CLAIM_INVALID`, `I14_PRIORITY_POLICY_MISSING`. Reopen on objective/claims/policy change. Acceptance: missing denominator stays open; unsupported AI direction is excluded; no ranking without criteria.

Optional proposed AI path: use the common-rules AI proposal contract, unexecuted and unapproved in A40. Keep evidence-linked candidates in a separate layer-three list with exact fact references, concise rationale, assumptions, counterevidence and limits. They are not owner-authored options, facts or decisions. No ranks/scores or automatic action. Human disposition is separate; existing button-only B7/B10 contracts acquire no new required reason fields.
