# I13 · Thương hiệu và đối thủ

Spec v1.0.0 · coverage `DRAFTED` · authority `PROPOSED_FROZEN_CONTENT_CODING` · implementation `NEW_CODE_IF_APPROVED` · readiness `NOT_ASSESSED` · execution/review `NOT_RUN`.

## Question and boundary

What claims, offers or themes appear in a frozen set of already existing brand/competitor content? This recipe preserves exact messages and source scope. It does not identify a competitor from co-occurrence, rank brands, or claim message prevalence outside the selected corpus.

## Inputs and procedure

Required: owner-defined brand/peer membership and research question; frozen corpus with source IDs/digests, channels, dates and sample inclusion rules; each content unit's `EvidenceRecordRef` and exact quote/image-text locator; brand identity as source declares it; versioned owner-approved coding scheme and coder/adjudication provenance. No new content collection or simulated consumer response.

1. Freeze which content types, channels, period and entity references are in corpus. If owner membership or sampling frame is absent, output an unranked located-source inventory only.
2. For each exact content unit, copy claim/offer wording and locator. Code message, offer and audience only with approved taxonomy; preserve negation, fine print, condition and context. A detected brand name is not sufficient to resolve corporate or competitor identity.
3. Deduplicate only repeated exact evidenceRecordRef values or a real stable source record key; identical text at different locators remains distinct. Retain alternate representations separately and do not sum reposts or cross-source duplicates without an approved identity rule. Count coded content units, not unique brands/people, absent stable IDs.
4. Compare two messages only if peer set, channel, unit, period and sampling frame match. Show source records and coding coverage; do not derive persuasion, awareness, sentiment or performance from content presence.

Missing claims remain unknown, not “no message.” Conflicts are both retained. Display order follows frozen owner peer order then source digest/locator; absent priority is unranked.

## Output, example and limits

Output: peer-set/codebook versions, corpus digest/frame, brand reference and basis, content unit/claim/offer, locator, date/channel, codes, adjudication, conflict and count basis. Toy fixture: two synthetic web-page spans from the frozen corpus mention “calcium + D3”; expected two located content records under that code, no market messaging share.

Blocked fixture: a search result appears near the target in a category but has no owner-approved competitor relation or stable entity reference. Expected `I13_PEER_IDENTITY_UNRESOLVED`; retain as an unclassified page, no competitor comparison.

Allowed: “In this frozen corpus, N located content units were coded X.” Cite each locator and sampling limits. Forbidden: brand rank, market awareness/prevalence, product superiority, audience response, sales impact or strategy recommendation.

AI role: suggest codes only under approved taxonomy and with exact excerpt; human adjudicates. Requires owner-declared peer scope and frozen corpus/coding inputs directly. May optionally reuse M07 peer context, I10 coded corpus and M13 provenance. I10 does not depend on I13. Open choices: peer definitions, channels, sampling frame, coding unit, taxonomy, duplicate/repost policy and comparisons. Blockers: `I13_CORPUS_UNFROZEN`, `I13_CONTENT_LOCATOR_MISSING`, `I13_PEER_IDENTITY_UNRESOLVED`, `I13_CODEBOOK_UNAPPROVED`. Reopen on source, peer or frame change. Acceptance: canonical title remains exact; unapproved peer is not ranked; sample frequency is explicitly corpus-only.

Record identity: apply the common-rules record-identity policy. Collapse only repeated exact evidenceRecordRef values or records proven identical by a stable source key. Identical wording at different row/span locators remains distinct; text hashes alone never authorize record deduplication. Corpus counts are coded records, not people.
