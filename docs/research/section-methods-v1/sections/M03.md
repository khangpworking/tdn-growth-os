# M03 · Quy mô và diễn biến

Spec v1.0.0 · coverage `DRAFTED` · authority `EXISTING_BOUNDED_SUBMETHOD` · implementation `EXISTS_A32_SINGLE_PREPARATION` · readiness `NOT_ASSESSED` · execution/review `NOT_RUN`.

## Question and boundary

What observed revenue and unit totals does one exact prepared Metric selection contain in ALL, WIDE and CORE? A32's approved bounded recipe calculates one prepared period. It does not calculate time-series change, growth, forecast, market share, unique product count, or market size. The word “diễn biến” in the canonical title does not add a time-series method.

A32 additionally requires no label issues for this recipe. |

No additional input is optional for A32. The calculation consumes no AI output. WIDE policy must be `exclude` for this recipe.

## Compatibility and procedure

1. Replay exact A30 preparation and recompute A31 from exact catalog bytes; require requested preparation/catalog/readiness digests to match and M03 readiness to be `READY_TO_CALCULATE`.
2. Verify M03 catalog identity is `metric-scope-packet-totals` v1.0.0. A32 checks `wideUnknownPolicy === exclude`, replayed normalized-input identity, zero label issues, calculated scope statuses, and canonical ALL/WIDE/CORE and comparison order. It does not compare multiple periods or add a timezone field; this one-period recipe has no time-series operation.
3. Calculate exact `metric-scope-v1`: ALL = all row indices; WIDE = every row except OUTSIDE and UNKNOWN; CORE = CORE_CANDIDATE only. For revenue and units in each scope, sum observed values with integer arithmetic; retain `observedCount`, `missingCount`, `nonExactCount`, and `complete`. `value=null` when there are no observed values. A subtotal with missing rows retains its observed sum and `complete=false`.
4. Emit membership comparisons ALL→WIDE and ALL→CORE. Revenue delta exists only when both revenues have no missing values; units delta only when each units total is complete. These are same-period membership differences. Bind exact preparation, readiness, input, method, catalog and rounding identities into the verified metric set; downstream chart/narrative consumers bind its `metricSetSha256`.

Dedup identity is `[platform, shopId, listingId]`; duplicate rows reject input. There is no growth denominator in this operation. Values are integer source units; no additional rounding. Deterministic order is ALL, WIDE, CORE; rows follow normalized input order. With any missing numeric observation, show the known subtotal plus missing count, never a complete total. Label issues block WIDE/CORE, not permission to relabel.

## Output, example and failure

Output is the A32 `VerifiedMetricSet`: exact scope/label policy, source and preparation identities, totals/counts/completeness, warnings and membership deltas. It contains totals and comparisons, not full group/composition arrays. A38 also replays the source-backed full Result against the pinned preparation and retained M03 before assembling the bounded sections. A32 source limits apply: normalized input only; missing ≠ zero; scopes overlap; UNKNOWN excluded from WIDE; listing ≠ unique product; membership difference ≠ growth or causality.

Synthetic rows: (A) CORE_CANDIDATE revenue 100; (B) UNKNOWN revenue observed zero; (C) OUTSIDE revenue missing. Expected ALL revenue `{value: "100", observedCount: 2, missingCount: 1, complete: false}`; WIDE `{value: "100", missingCount: 0}`; CORE `{value: "100", missingCount: 0}`. UNKNOWN remains visible in ALL, excluded from WIDE; the difference is not growth.

Blocked example: one label is stale or missing. A31 cannot declare M03 ready; calculation stops with `M03_LABELS_NOT_FROZEN`, without imputing membership or returning zero.

## Claims, review and dependencies

Allowed: “Within this normalized selected export and period, the observed subtotal is …; X rows are missing.” Link numeric phrases to exact verified-output pointers and the preparation/source locator through retained input/result artifacts. A total has no business denominator; row coverage remains explicit. Forbidden: temporal trend, whole-market size/share, demand, causality, forecast, representative population, or additive ALL/WIDE/CORE totals.

AI role: none for calculation or numeric description. A later separately governed interpretation may comment only on eligible claims and remains unreviewed. Owner verifies source meaning and report use. Depends on A30→A31; downstream to M04, I03, I17 and A27 chart bundle. It never depends on M01.

Proposed blockers: `M03_PREPARATION_INVALID`, `M03_READINESS_MISMATCH`, `M03_LABELS_NOT_FROZEN`, `M03_SCOPE_INCOMPATIBLE`. Reopen on changed source, label/codebook, period/scope, policy, method or digest. Acceptance: missing versus zero behavior above (metric calculator owns); stale label blocks exact recipe (A31/A32 own); same preparation/catalog returns same `metricSetSha256` (A32 verifier owns). No extra tests required by this document.
