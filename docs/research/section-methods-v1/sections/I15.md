# I15 · Định hướng chiến lược

Spec v1.0.0 · coverage `DRAFTED` · authority `PROPOSED_STRATEGY_DECISION_PACKET; CHOICE_POLICY_UNRESOLVED` · implementation `HUMAN_DECISION_REQUIRED` · readiness `NOT_ASSESSED` · execution/review `NOT_RUN`.

## Question and boundary

What owner-authored strategy options and constraints can be compared against exact evidence, and what remains a human decision? This recipe assembles a decision packet; no automatic strategy choice, ranking, score or action is allowed.

## Inputs and procedure

Required for an evidence packet: exact I01 owner brief; owner-authored strategy alternatives if any; exact reviewed claim refs and counterevidence; known capability/cost/time/risk constraints with source/owner and version; explicit unset fields. Optional: owner decision criteria/weights, risk tolerance, horizon and review date. Missing constraints remain unknown, not zero.

1. Replay owner brief and every referenced claim/artifact. Preserve evidence layers and periods/denominators. Exclude invalid references from comparison and surface why.
2. Create one row per owner-supplied alternative. If the owner supplied none, keep ownerOptions empty. A separately proposed aiCandidates list may contain evidence-linked drafts for human review; it is not an owner-option list. Map each claim/constraint to each alternative only where the link is explicitly supplied or directly present in source.
3. Show tradeoffs as exact comparable facts or `UNKNOWN`; do not normalize heterogeneous cost, capability or risk fields to a common score. If criteria/weights are not approved, leave rank and preferred option null.
4. A human owner records a separate authorized decision. Optional future report-review rationale, cited assumptions, rejected alternatives and reconsideration context may link separately; none is a new mandatory B7/B10 field. This is not an execution authorization.

Dedup exact claim ID+digest. Ties follow owner order; no implicit “do nothing” priority unless owner lists it. No unsupported market sizing, return, capability or budget assumption.

## Output, example and limits

Output: owner brief, alternative list, exact evidence/constraints per alternative, conflicts, missing fields, comparison policy ref, `preferredOption=null` until human decision and decision ref when present. Toy fixture: owner supplies options A/B, one verified cost for A and a missing cost for B. Expected the matrix shows A's cost and B `UNKNOWN`; no B cost is set to zero, and no ranking is emitted.

Blocked fixture: AI generated “enter premium segment” with no owner-supplied option/claim. Expected `I15_OPTION_NOT_OWNER_SUPPLIED`; no recommendation enters the packet.

Allowed: “The packet has alternatives A/B, known constraint C and unknown cost for B.” Forbidden: preferred strategy, weighted score, competitive position, guaranteed return, launch instruction or risk acceptance without authorization.

AI role: it may format exact claims and optionally draft unranked strategy candidates under the proposed shared contract; no scoring, choice or decision authority. Human reviews and decides. Requires an exact owner brief (may reuse I01) and relevant evidence/constraints; may reuse I14/M11 outputs. I15 feeds M12, not the reverse. A previous human decision may be optional historical context but never a live M12 prerequisite or strategy-evidence source. Open choices: strategy horizon, options, criteria, weights, capabilities, cost, risk appetite, owner and review trigger. Proposed blockers: `I15_OWNER_BRIEF_MISSING`, `I15_OPTIONS_UNAPPROVED`, `I15_TRADEOFF_INPUT_MISSING`, `I15_CHOICE_POLICY_MISSING`. Reopen on brief, option, constraint or claim change. Acceptance: unknown cost never becomes zero; no owner alternative leaves ownerOptions empty and any AI candidates separately unreviewed; missing criteria means no ranking.

Optional proposed AI path: use the common-rules AI proposal contract, unexecuted and unapproved in A40. Keep evidence-linked candidates in a separate layer-three list with exact fact references, concise rationale, assumptions, counterevidence and limits. They are not owner-authored options, facts or decisions. No ranks/scores or automatic action. Human disposition is separate; existing button-only B7/B10 contracts acquire no new required reason fields.
