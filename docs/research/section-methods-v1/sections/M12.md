# M12 · Hành động

Spec v1.0.0 · coverage `DRAFTED` · authority `PROPOSED_OWNER_DECISION_PACKET; ACTION_POLICY_UNRESOLVED` · implementation `HUMAN_DECISION_REQUIRED` · readiness `NOT_ASSESSED` · execution/review `NOT_RUN`.

## Question and boundary

What decision does the owner need to make, what exact evidence and constraints inform it, and who records the chosen action? The automated procedure prepares a decision packet. It does not select or execute an action.

## Inputs and procedure

Required for a packet: owner-supplied objective and decision question; exact evidence claim refs plus limitations/conflicts; action options if the owner has supplied them; accountable role/decision authority; known budget/capability/time/risk constraints. Optional: owner-written decision criteria, due date and reconsideration trigger. Missing fields remain unset, not completed by agent.

1. Replay exact cited claims and preserve source, computation, AI interpretation and user decision layers separately.
2. For each owner-provided option, record evidence links, dependencies, resource constraints, unresolved assumptions and reversibility exactly as supplied. If owner options are absent, preserve an empty owner-option list. The separately proposed AI path may hold unreviewed candidates in a different list; none becomes an owner option automatically.
3. Show options side by side in owner-provided order. Only calculate comparable values whose formulas and denominators are approved; do not create weighted score, rank, ROI or preferred option by default.
4. Await a human decision recorded under the actual authority and constraints; reference the separate authorized decision (chosen option/defer, actor/time as allowed by its existing contract). Optional future report-review rationale/citations may link separately; they are not mandatory B7/B10 fields. This method does not perform that action.

No AI-generated owner, budget, capability, urgency, risk tolerance or success threshold. A missing owner is an explicit blocker. No option tie-break or auto-selection.

## Output, example and failure

Output before decision: question, evidence packet, owner options or empty list, comparison policy ref or null, missing constraints and `decisionState=OPEN`. After authorized human action: exact chosen option/defer, decision reference and review state. Toy fixture: owner supplies options A/B and two conflicting cited claims; packet shows both options and conflict, `chosen=null`.

Blocked fixture: an AI draft says “launch now” but there is no owner authority, objective or source claim. Expected `M12_OWNER_AUTHORITY_MISSING`; no action is issued.

Allowed: “Decision remains open; owner inputs needed are …”. Forbidden: recommendation, task assignment, purchase, campaign launch, funding/supplier choice or claim that action has been approved unless separate human decision exists.

AI role: it may format exact claims and, under the separate proposed contract, draft unranked action options for review. It cannot choose, prioritize or execute them. Human owner retains all decision authority. Required inputs are the owner question and relevant reviewed claims. Optional I15 strategy context is upstream of this action packet; M12 is not an upstream prerequisite of I15. Open decisions: choice criteria, authority, constraints, reversibility, risk acceptance and reconsideration. Proposed blockers: `M12_OWNER_AUTHORITY_MISSING`, `M12_OBJECTIVE_MISSING`, `M12_OPTION_POLICY_MISSING`, `M12_EVIDENCE_CONFLICT_UNREVIEWED`. Reopen on claim/constraint/decision changes. Acceptance: missing options remain empty; a conflicting claim is visible; no write/execute step without separate human authorization.

Optional proposed AI path: use the common-rules AI proposal contract, unexecuted and unapproved in A40. Keep evidence-linked candidates in a separate layer-three list with exact fact references, concise rationale, assumptions, counterevidence and limits. They are not owner-authored options, facts or decisions. No ranks/scores or automatic action. Human disposition is separate; existing button-only B7/B10 contracts acquire no new required reason fields.
