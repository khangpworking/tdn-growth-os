## Adopted scope

1. **Source-bound descriptive market v1 (D01-D04).** M05 keeps source measure
   names and aggregates only compatible, demonstrably disjoint observations.
   M06 preserves source-stated inventory/status. M07 compares owner-declared
   peers side by side. M09 inventories located, dated attributed events. No
   inferred demand, complete market size/supply, unique-product assumption,
   inferred stock/share, composite score, rank, best option or causal claim.
2. **Located qualitative coding (D06-D07).** Adopt the concrete
   `located-evidence-v1-draft` codebook and literal mappings as v1. Pin exact
   revision/bytes before execution and keep its ID/name consistent. Clear
   mappings need no additional per-row owner approval; ambiguous cases and new
   codes require disposition. Preserve negation, conditions, hearsay, locators
   and raw text without invented identities or joins. Corpus-only n/N requires
   frozen membership, unit, inclusion rules, period, codebook and a complete
   denominator with coding complete. Report exclusions, unreadable, uncoded,
   pending and multi-code records. Pending AI/unresolved labels are not accepted
   counts, zero or negatives and do not disappear from raw membership or the
   denominator. Incomplete coding blocks final code ratios; provisional accepted
   counts must show coverage and partial status. Reviewed UNCLEAR/UNCODED is a
   completed disposition. No population/customer/market inference.
3. **Optional retained AI candidate design (D12).** Adopt the closed type/section
   mapping for SUMMARY_DRAFT, HYPOTHESIS, OPPORTUNITY_DIRECTION, STRATEGY_OPTION,
   ACTION_OPTION, DRIVER_HYPOTHESIS and CODE_SUGGESTION. Retain exact claim refs,
   locators, assumptions, counterevidence, UNKNOWN/gaps/limits and attempt/config/
   prompt/output/validation metadata. Numeric facts must be bound and rendered
   deterministically. Human semantic review remains necessary; pointer checks
   cannot guarantee truth or eliminate hallucination. Owner options and AI
   candidates remain separate; regeneration creates a new retained attempt,
   while replay returns the original output. No hidden chain-of-thought or
   automatic choice. This adopts design, not model/provider activation. Code
   and driver suggestions retain their type/section bindings and interpretation
   layer after review. B7/B10 remain button-only without mandatory reasons or
   new reviewer-role infrastructure.
4. **Defer advanced analyses.** Production forecasts, population inference,
   causal/effectiveness claims and advanced score/rank/threshold policies remain
   disabled. M10 eligibility/blocker reporting may be implemented; the proposed
   last-observed/MAE diagnostic needs separate approval/activation and run inputs.
   I11 retains descriptive group inventories; I12 separates presence, exposure
   and outcomes without conversion claims; I16 supports design-only or exact
   existing protocol/results. No new surveys, recruitment or experiments.
