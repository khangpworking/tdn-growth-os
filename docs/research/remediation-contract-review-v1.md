## Accepted implementation scope

- M03 gets a new temporal contract, separate from the single-period Metric
  recipe. Inventory is allowed with exact source linkage. Sum needs verified
  additive period-flow semantics, compatible fixed membership, disjoint full
  target coverage and exact complete contributions. Change additionally needs
  equal durations and chronological non-overlap; relative change needs a
  positive baseline. Unknown time boundaries allow inventory only. No inferred
  seasonality, forecast, market totals or automatic newest-snapshot selection.
- M08 gets `generic-quote-unit-v1`, separate from tablet P4. Each quote retains
  offer/variant identity and linkage, price state/conditions, source locators,
  observation-time state, count and selected NET/DRAINED basis. Per-item needs a
  positive homogeneous physical-item count; per-100g needs positive, explicitly
  linked mass. Only kg/g conversion is supported. Keep reduced rational results
  and two-decimal half-even display. Missing operands block their own operation,
  not the inventory. Range prices are not reduced to the minimum or midpoint;
  declared denominators remain scenario assumptions. No cost/margin, volume,
  battery or performance denominator, bundle allocation, ranking or checkout
  assertion without corresponding evidence.
- Synthesis needs a new tagged input/packet adapter, preserving source,
  calculation/coding, AI interpretation and authenticated human decision as
  separate layers. It must not coerce descriptive or located output into a
  legacy FACT schema. No AI attempt or business decision is enabled by this
  review; unresolved directions, priority and actions remain unset.
- Generic review intake retains all returned raw records with exact listing,
  page and row lineage. Product-use admission is a separate view and cannot
  remove the raw evidence. Category-specific vocabulary and codebook revisions
  must be pinned before admission/coding. Hearsay and negation stay explicit;
  identical text at distinct IDs is not a duplicate. Conflicting same-ID records
  stay unresolved. Pending admission/coding cannot disappear from coverage to
  manufacture a complete denominator. The calcium adapter and its historical
  hash remain unchanged.

schema, contradictory source roles and corrupt lineage retain their existing
fail-closed boundaries. This versioned clarification authorizes changing the
old whole-input rejection assertions for these denominator values only.
