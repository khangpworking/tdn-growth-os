## Admission notes

- Seven rows name bounded existing code: M02, M03, M04, M08, M13, I03, I17. Their method authority stops at the listed sub-scope; no broader recipe is implied.
- Other rows describe proposals, safe inventories, exact-input gates, or human tasks. They are not implemented by this packet. `BUSINESS_REVIEWED_PROPOSAL` describes review of a proposed method only; it is not `OWNER_APPROVED`.
- Only exact relevant dependencies are needed. A missing unrelated section does not block an evidence inventory. Optional outputs do not become mandatory dependencies.
- Every source-backed example requires an internal exact locator. Identical text at different locators remains distinct. No stable identity means no cross-source join or people/entity count.
- Method-specific population, causality, forecast, effectiveness, ranking, conversion and action claims remain blocked unless the matrix row names an exact reviewed path and required owner authority.
