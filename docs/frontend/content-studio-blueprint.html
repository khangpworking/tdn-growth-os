Content Studio Blueprint

The agreed UI for Content Studio inside TDN Growth OS. All content shown is a synthetic example.

Thương hiệu & sản phẩm→Chiến dịch→1 Insight→2 Big Idea→3 Góc + mục đích→4 Caption & Poster
  • Products and services live in the brand catalog, with tiers (Go / Plus / Pro).
  • Each campaign writes its own customer, pain point and insight.
  • Develop several Big Ideas or Angles; codes A, A1, A1·P show every branch.
  • An Angle can carry several purposes: suggested tags plus your own via “+”.
  • Every step and the package: library prompt or your own. Style and length: campaign default, batch, or per package.
  • Brand info shows by purpose; contact details are added by the system, never by AI.
  • Delete from “⋯”; restorable for 24 hours. AI edits are proposals you apply.
