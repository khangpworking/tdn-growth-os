# Research A35: deterministic M03 factual narrative

A35 renders six Vietnamese factual paragraphs from the exact A34 evidence
envelope: one paragraph for each ALL/WIDE/CORE scope, one for each membership
sensitivity comparison, and one fixed limitations paragraph.

The renderer copies only cited claim values. A runtime guard rejects any numeric
token not present in the paragraph's cited claims. Missing observations produce
“chưa đủ dữ liệu để tính”; observed zero remains an explicit zero. The result is
content-identified and marked as a deterministic factual draft that has not been
approved by the OWNER.

This is intentionally code-written prose, not AI prose. It removes stochastic
variation from the quantitative account. A future AI layer may add bounded
interpretation or hypotheses only under the separate business methodology,
citations, validation and human review.
