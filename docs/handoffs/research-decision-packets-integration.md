# M11/M12/I15: paired source-backed packet integration

Date: 2026-10-04. Unreleased working tree on `0116091`; not the Fedora operator.

## Delivered boundary

The report owner now builds and retains M11/M12 packets in Market and I15 in
Insight. Insight is rendered first so Market can pin that exact sibling's
semantic artifact. Historical reads verify the same pair, its frozen sources
and reconstructed packet; they do not select the newest Insight or recode old
reviews. A corrupt paired raw source fails replay in both reports that use it.

Claims retain their source section, attribution, literal spans, qualifiers,
scope and measurement limits. I14's generated text is not reused as source.
Market and review evidence do not acquire a common denominator, listing,
person, period or independent-source status merely by sharing a report pair.

The renderer exposes evidence and missing owner fields. No option is selected,
ranked or authorized. These outputs remain inventories, not complete analyses.
No schema migration, production model call or deployment was performed.
